
# Defining the Use Case

## 1. Introduction

Defining a use case is the first stage of the Generative AI application lifecycle.

This phase establishes the foundation for the project by:

- Defining the problem to be solved.
- Gathering relevant requirements.
- Aligning stakeholder expectations.

Teams analyze the problem, consult subject matter experts, and convert business needs into technical requirements.

## 2. Business Use Cases

A business use case is a structured description of how a system or process should behave from the perspective of an actor or stakeholder.

It communicates the functional requirements of a system or process.

### Parts of a Use Case

1. **Use Case Name:** A short and descriptive name identifying the use case.

2. **Brief Description:** A summary of the purpose and objective of the use case.

3. **Actors:** People or external systems that interact with the system, such as customers or employees.

4. **Preconditions:** Conditions that must be true before the use case begins.

5. **Basic Flow (Main Success Scenario):** Step-by-step actions followed to complete the use case successfully.

6. **Alternative Flows (Extensions):** Alternative situations, errors, or exceptional conditions and how the system handles them.

7. **Postconditions:** Conditions that must be true after successful completion.

8. **Business Rules:** Policies, constraints, or regulations governing the system.

9. **Nonfunctional Requirements:** Requirements related to performance, security, and usability.

10. **Assumptions:** Conditions assumed to be true for the use case to work.

11. **Notes or Additional Information:** Extra details useful for understanding or implementing the use case.

## 3. Addressing Business Use Cases with Generative AI

### Key Metrics

1. **Cost Savings:** Reduction in labor costs and expenses through automation and efficiency.

2. **Time Savings:** Reduction in the time required to complete tasks.

3. **Quality Improvement:** Improvement in the accuracy, coherence, and creativity of outputs.

4. **Customer Satisfaction:** Improvement in customer experience, satisfaction scores, and feedback.

5. **Productivity Gains:** Increase in output, reduced errors, and faster task completion.

### Approaches to Address Business Problems

1. **Process Automation:** Automates repetitive and time-consuming tasks, improving efficiency and reducing costs.

2. **Augmented Decision-Making:** Provides insights and recommendations to improve business decisions.

3. **Personalization and Customization:** Creates customized content, products, and experiences for users.

4. **Creative Content Generation:** Generates text, images, videos, and audio for marketing, advertising, and education.

5. **Exploratory Analysis and Innovation:** Generates new ideas and solutions to encourage innovation.

**Note:** The choice of metrics and approaches depends on the business problem, industry, and organizational goals.
